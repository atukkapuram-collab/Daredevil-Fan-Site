# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS in one folder, no framework, no build step (set by the user's brief).

## Users

Primary: the owner's portfolio audience — designers, design engineers and recruiters who judge interaction craft. They arrive from a portfolio link, on desktop or phone, curious and short on time, and decide within seconds whether the work is distinctive. Daredevil fans are a secondary audience who should find the content accurate and affectionate.

## Product Purpose

An unofficial fan tribute to Marvel's Daredevil that doubles as an interaction-design showcase. Success: the visitor plays with the central interaction, reads at least one section, and follows the single action — exploring Hell's Kitchen.

## Positioning

The page is experienced the way Matt Murdock experiences the world: mostly dark, revealed by sensing. Content surfaces through radar-like ripples from the visitor's own taps and pointer, not by default visibility. A generic superhero fan page cannot claim this mechanism.

## Operating Context

Visited from a portfolio link; viewed on desktop (hover + click) and phones (tap only). Must be fully usable without hover and with reduced motion.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections: Who he is; Essential runs (reading guide); Hell's Kitchen (the single action's destination); Rogues & allies.
- Small JS "pop-out" graphic moments are wanted, but nothing overbearing.
- Hard legal constraint: no Marvel logos, official art, cover art, stills or screenshots. Only original type, original illustration (SVG/CSS/canvas) and real photographs of the Hell's Kitchen neighborhood (openly licensed, credited).
- Footer must carry an unofficial fan-site disclaimer.

## Brand Commitments

- Concept is fixed by the owner: "radar sense" — near-black page; tap or hover emits a ripple that reveals content; braille-dot headline.
- Characters and trademarks belong to Marvel; the site never implies affiliation.

## Evidence on Hand

No assets provided. Neighborhood photos must come from openly licensed sources with credit. No testimonials, stats or quotes may be invented; comic runs are cited by creator names and years only.

## Product Principles

1. The interaction is the thesis: reveal through sensing, never through decoration alone.
2. Content stays reachable: dark-by-default must never mean unreadable for keyboard, touch, screen-reader or reduced-motion users.
3. Restraint over spectacle: one signature effect, small supporting moments.
4. Respect the source without borrowing its assets.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

A tribute to a blind hero must not lock content behind a visual-only effect: real braille-reading users and screen readers get full text; all revealed content is in the DOM and readable; reduced-motion gets a static, fully lit fallback.
