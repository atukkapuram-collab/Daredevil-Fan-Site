# taste-skill notes

Reading of the brief: an interaction-craft portfolio piece dressed as an unofficial Daredevil tribute.
One action: Explore Hell's Kitchen. Stack: static HTML/CSS/JS.
Tooling: the skill's scripts (draw/inspect/assets/lint) are not installed in this environment — direction
was set by the user's brief instead of a dealt draw; checks are done with the built-in browser.

## Inventory (things that belong to this subject and no other)

1. **The radar sense** — Matt perceives the world as sound bouncing back: a pulse, then shapes in outline.
2. **Blindness from a chemical spill** — a truck, a boy pushing an old man out of the way, the canister. Origin as a single moment.
3. **Braille** — six-dot cells; Matt reads it with his fingertips. The name "DAREDEVIL" can be set as real braille.
4. **Hell's Kitchen, Manhattan** — West 34th to 59th, 8th Avenue to the Hudson. Tenement walk-ups, fire escapes, the elevated rail turned High Line nearby, the Hudson piers, 9th/10th Ave bodegas and bars.
5. **Nelson & Murdock** — a two-person storefront law office; a hand-lettered window.
6. **Fogwell's Gym** — Battlin' Jack Murdock, the boxer father; a gym bell, a heavy bag. "Get up."
7. **The billy club** — a cane that splits into a weapon; the one physical object.
8. **The colour**: a single red against night. The red is the suit; the night is his world.
9. **Heartbeats** — he hears lies in a heartbeat. A pulse rhythm, ~60–100 bpm.
10. **Catholic guilt** — the church, the confessional, Father Lantom. Candlelight.
11. **Rooftops at night** — he works between water towers.
12. **Essential runs** carry real years: Miller 1979–83, Born Again 1986, Nocenti 1986–91, Smith "Guardian Devil" 1998, Bendis/Maleev 2001–06, Brubaker 2006–09, Waid 2011–15, Zdarsky 2019–23.

## Argument (claims → proof)

- Hero: "The world, felt in echoes" → the radar interaction itself; braille headline that resolves to letters.
- Who he is: a blind lawyer who sees by sound → three plain facts (the accident, the senses, the double life).
- Essential runs: forty years of great runs, where to start → the list with creators and years.
- Rogues & allies: his world is people → short character entries, revealed by the pulse.
- Hell's Kitchen: the real neighborhood under the fiction → real photographs with street captions; the single action lands here.
- Footer: unofficial tribute → disclaimer + photo credits.

## Idea sentence

> On this page, **Matt Murdock's radar sense — sound striking the city and bouncing back** — becomes
> **a ripple from the visitor's own tap or pointer that lights the near-black page for a moment, revealing the content it touches.**

Competitor test: put another hero in the sentence (Batman, Spider-Man) — it breaks; only Daredevil
perceives by echo. Passes.

Supporting: the headline "DAREDEVIL" is set as six-dot braille cells that resolve into Latin letters as
the first echo passes over them.

## The one live thing

The echo. Tap anywhere (thumb on phone, pointer/click on desktop): a ring expands from the point and
everything it crosses brightens, then fades back to a dim-but-readable resting state. A small
heartbeat in the corner can be toggled into "sense mode". Fallback without JS / reduced motion: the
page is fully lit and static.

## Planned overrides of house rules (flag in report)

- **Thin rules**: echo rings are thin strokes — brief mandates the ripple. Brief > house rule.
- **Gradients**: radial-gradient used only as an alpha *mask* for the reveal (never a visible colour gradient). Brief > house rule.
- **Glows**: none planned; red light uses flat colour.
